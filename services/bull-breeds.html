Questions owners actually ask

Will you actually take my dog?

Yes. We don’t work from a breed list or make decisions based on appearance or paperwork. We welcome dogs based on the individual: their temperament, how they are on the day, and whether they can be safely and comfortably groomed in our salon.

Does he need to wear a muzzle?

Let us know when you book. If your dog is more comfortable wearing a muzzle, or you simply prefer them to wear one during their groom, that is absolutely fine.

If a muzzle is needed, we will always discuss it with you beforehand. It does not change the way your dog is treated: their comfort, dignity and wellbeing remain our priority throughout the groom.

Nothing is sprung on you. Everything is agreed in advance.

Can we come and meet you first?

Yes, and for a dog who has had a bad experience elsewhere it is often the best way to start. A short visit with no grooming, so the room and the person are already familiar next time.

Is there a surcharge because he is big or strong?

Not for being big or strong. Price follows size, coat condition and how much time the groom takes, exactly as it does for every other dog.

He was turned away by another salon. What is different here?

We don’t make decisions based on size, breed or appearance alone. Large and powerful dogs are welcome at The Fur Salon, and Anna is confident and experienced in handling them.

At the same time, every dog is assessed as an individual. We are happy to work with big dogs, but we are not a specialist service for dogs with aggression or unsafe behaviour.

For us, it’s simple: being big is never the problem.

My dog has a short coat. Does he even need grooming?

More than most owners think. Short coats shed constantly, and the skin underneath still needs washing, checking and treating. De-shedding, nails, ears and teeth make a visible difference to a short-coated dog.
